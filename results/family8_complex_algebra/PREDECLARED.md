## 1. Scientific Object

The paper investigates neural operator surrogates under sequential composition. Given a dictionary of single-operator neural surrogates $\{S_1, \dots, S_m\}$, each trained to emulate one physical operator's flow map over a discrete leg duration $\tau = 1.0$ (100 steps of $\Delta t = 0.01$, estimand stride $K=10$), we compose them to emulate arbitrary operator words:
$$w = O_{i_k} \circ \dots \circ O_{i_1}$$

Two prior results established in 1-D dynamics:
1. **Composed-operator degradation and BROAD-S repair** (Families 1c/1e): When sequential composition drives intermediate states off the single-operator training support, narrow surrogates degrade severely ($R \gg 1$). State-space broadening at fixed sample budget ($n=640$ trajectories) restores in-support accuracy ($P_3 \le 0.25$).
2. **Failure of test-time splitting** (Family 6): At equal sample budget, test-time operator splitting (Serrano et al., 2026) cannot recover the composed endpoint because it lacks the out-of-distribution state support.

**Family 8 extends these findings to a complex 2-D setting with a 4-operator dictionary, longer words (length 2 and 3), and introduces ALGEBRA RECOVERY:**
Given an unlabelled trajectory generated by an unknown operator word $w$, identify the generating word $w$ (and its ordering) via composition search over the surrogate dictionary.

**The central scientific hypothesis**:
A state-space-broadened dictionary (`BROAD-S`) successfully recovers the true operator word on complex 2-D multi-operator dynamics where a narrow dictionary fails, and its composed rollouts track the true composed operator for words of length 2 and 3.

## 2. Operator Dictionary ($m=4$, Mutually Noncommuting)

All operators operate on the 2-D periodic domain $[0, 1)^2$ with $\Delta t = 0.01$, leg duration $\tau = 100 \times \Delta t = 1.0$, and estimand stride $K = 10$ ($\tau_{\text{estimand}} = 0.10$).

| Symbol | Operator | Governing Equation / Implementation | Key Parameters | Conservation / Drift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D** | Diffusion | $\partial_t u = \kappa \nabla^2 u$ (`ex.stepper.Diffusion`) | $\kappa = 0.01$ | Conservative ($\langle u \rangle$ invariant) |
| **A** | Cellular Advection | $\partial_t u = - v \cdot \nabla u$ (`CellularAdvectionStepper`) | Fixed cellular flow: $\psi = \frac{U_0}{2\pi}\sin(2\pi x)\sin(2\pi y)$, $U_0 = 1.0$; ETDRK4, dealiasing $2/3$ | Conservative ($\nabla \cdot v = 0$, $\langle u \rangle$ invariant) |
| **R** | Fisher-KPP Reaction | $\partial_t u = r u (1 - u)$ (`ex.stepper.reaction.FisherKPP`) | $r = 1.0$, diffusivity $0.0$; ETDRK2, dealiasing $2/3$ | Upward mean drift ($\partial_t \langle u \rangle > 0$) |
| **G** | Gray-Scott 1-species | $\partial_t u = F(1 - u) - u(1 - u)^2$ (`GeneralPolynomialStepper`) | $F = 0.04$, linear $(-1.04, 0, 0)$, poly $(0.04, 0, 2, -1)$; ETDRK2, dealiasing $2/3$ | Downward mean drift ($\partial_t \langle u \rangle < 0$) |

### Cellular Velocity Field Definition (A)
The streamfunction is:
$$\psi(x, y) = \frac{U_0}{2\pi} \sin(2\pi x) \sin(2\pi y), \quad U_0 = 1.0$$
The divergence-free velocity field is:
$$v_x(x, y) = -\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial y} = -U_0 \sin(2\pi x) \cos(2\pi y)$$
$$v_y(x, y) = \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial x} = U_0 \cos(2\pi x) \sin(2\pi y)$$
Because $\nabla \cdot v = \partial_x v_x + \partial_y v_y = 0$, the advection term satisfies $\int v \cdot \nabla u \, dx \, dy = \int \nabla \cdot (v u) \, dx \, dy = 0$.
Integration: ETDRK order 4 with Orszag 2/3 dealiasing rule. At $N=64, dx=1/64, dt=0.01$, CFL $= U_0 dt / dx = 0.64 \le 1.0$, guaranteeing numerical stability and exact mean conservation.

### G Fallback Rule
If Gray-Scott $G$ fails the pre-screen gates (G1, G2, order contrast), it is replaced by Allen-Cahn (`ex.stepper.reaction.AllenCahn`, diffusivity $0.005$, first order $1.0$, third order $-1.0$). If both fail, $G$ is dropped and the dictionary operates with $m=3$ members $\{D, A, R\}$. (Pre-screen verification confirms $G$ exhibits dynamic range $> 600 \gg 100$ and survives, while Allen-Cahn exhibits dynamic range $22.9 < 100$).

## 3. Truth-Only Pre-Screen Protocol

Before fitting any neural surrogate, a truth-only pre-screen (governed identically to Family 1b) is executed on all 12 ordered pairs of distinct dictionary members and the length-3 words.

### Pre-Screen Gates
1. **G1: Support Shift**:
   - The switch states $u_{\text{sw}}$ generated by Leg 1 must lie off the narrow training support of the receiving operator.
   - Evaluated by spatial-mean support overlap (`overlap == 0.0` and `gap > 0.0`) or in the PCA-99 Mahalanobis basis on flattened fields (out-of-support coverage $> 0$).
2. **G2: Dynamic Range**:
   - $\text{DR} = \frac{\text{persistence}_{\text{off}}}{e_{\text{on, ref}}} \ge 100.0$, where $e_{\text{on, ref}} = 1.92247 \times 10^{-4}$ (the reference on-support FNO error from Family 1 / Family 7).
3. **Noncommuting (Order Contrast)**:
   - For an ordered pair $(O_1, O_2)$, the order contrast between $O_2 \circ O_1$ and $O_1 \circ O_2$:
     $$\text{contrast}(O_1, O_2) = \text{median}\left(\frac{\|O_2(O_1(u_0)) - O_1(O_2(u_0))\|_2}{\|O_2(O_1(u_0))\|_2}\right) > 0.05$$
     or max-absolute contrast $\max |O_2(O_1(u_0)) - O_1(O_2(u_0))| > 0.05$.
   - Any pair or member failing G1, G2, or order contrast is recorded in `results/PRESCREEN.json`.

## 5. Neural Operator Architecture and Training Protocol

### Architecture: FNO-2D (Equinox)
Identical to Family 7 (`run_family7.py`):
- Modes: $12 \times 12$
- Channels / Width: 32
- Layers: 4 Fourier blocks with GELU activations
- Channel Projection: 64
- Inputs: 3 channels ($u(x, y)$ plus 2-D coordinate grid $(x, y)$)
- Output: 1 channel ($\hat{u}(x, y)$)
- Total Parameters: **2,365,825**

### Training Hyperparameters
- Number of steps: 8,000
- Batch size: 64
- Optimizer: Custom Adam ($\beta_1 = 0.9, \beta_2 = 0.999, \epsilon = 10^{-8}$)
- Learning rate schedule: Cosine decay from $\text{LR}_{\text{high}} = 10^{-3}$ to $\text{LR}_{\text{low}} = 10^{-5}$
- Loss function: Relative $L_2$ norm per batch: $\frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \frac{\|\hat{u}_i - u_i\|_2}{\|u_i\|_2}$

### Conditions: NARROW vs BROAD-S (Sample Budget $n = 640$ Fixed)
1. **NARROW Condition**:
   - Training ICs drawn from the member's narrow window: uniform offset in $[0.4865, 0.5171]$, amplitude $0.175$, cutoff 5 (`MASTER_SEED + 1 = 20260906`).
   - 640 trajectories rolled 100 steps under that operator. Pairs extracted at stride $K=10$ ($640 \times 91 = 58,240$ pairs).
2. **BROAD-S Condition (Family 1e Sampler over Union of Switch States)**:
   - Evaluates the truth rollouts of ALL words in the tested word list.
   - Collects intermediate switch states at leg transition points ($t = \tau, 2\tau$).
   - Constructs a stratified training IC corpus of $n = 640$ initial states:
     - 25% (160 units) base ICs ($m=0$)
     - 75% (480 units) uniformly drawn from the multi-operator switch-state union pool.
   - Each dictionary member surrogate $S_O$ is trained on 100-step rollouts from this broadened IC pool.
   - Sample count is strictly conserved: exactly $n=640$ trajectories per operator.

### Seeds
5 seeds ($0, 1, 2, 3, 4$). Reported individually and summarized by median and interquartile range.

## 6. Endpoints and Primary Gates

### Endpoint 1 (E1): Composed-Rollout Degradation and Broadening Repair
- For each word $w$ in the tested list, evaluate composed rollouts over held-out evaluation units ($N_{\text{eval}} = 32$).
- Measure rollout degradation $R = e_{\text{off}} / e_{\text{on}}$ for both NARROW and BROAD-S.
- **Primary Gate P3**:
  $$\text{Repair Ratio} = \frac{\text{median}(R_{\text{broadS}})}{\text{median}(R_{\text{narrow}})} \le 0.25$$
  aggregated across all tested words (primary) and reported per individual word.

### Endpoint 2 (E2): Operator-Algebra Recovery Search
- For each held-out truth trajectory generated by an unknown word in the list ($N_{\text{heldout}} \ge 20$ trajectories per word):
- Perform an exhaustive composition search over all candidate words of length $k \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ using:
  1. The NARROW surrogate dictionary
  2. The BROAD-S surrogate dictionary
- Each candidate word $w_{\text{cand}}$ is rolled out autoregressively from $u_{\text{true}}(0)$ and scored by trajectory relative $L_2$ error:
  $$\mathcal{L}(w_{\text{cand}}) = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^T \frac{\|u_{\text{pred}}(t) - u_{\text{true}}(t)\|_2}{\|u_{\text{true}}(t)\|_2}$$
- Select top-1 predicted word $w^* = \arg\min \mathcal{L}(w_{\text{cand}})$.
- Report:
  1. Top-1 Word Identification Accuracy ($\% w^* == w_{\text{true}}$)
  2. Order Accuracy ($\%$ correct sequence order among matched operators)
- **Primary Gate ALG-1**:
  - $\text{Accuracy}(\text{BROAD-S}) \ge 0.80$ (at least 80% top-1 recovery)
  - $\text{Accuracy}(\text{BROAD-S}) - \text{Accuracy}(\text{NARROW}) \ge 0.30$ (at least 30% absolute improvement over narrow dictionary).

### Endpoint 3 (E3): Serrano-Style Test-Time Operator Splitting
- On length-3 words ($R \circ A \circ D, D \circ A \circ R, G \circ D \circ A$), run test-time operator splitting with the narrow dictionary as the comparison arm (reusing Family 6's method specification `family6_serrano_baseline/METHOD_SPEC.md`).
- Formally described as *test-time operator splitting* (never attributing the words Lie or Strang to Serrano et al.).
- Demonstrates that test-time splitting of narrow operators cannot compensate for off-support state-space shift.

## 9. Strict Ordering Device and Verification Protocol

1. **Pre-screen**: Truth-only screen evaluates G1, G2, and order contrast, recording `results/PRESCREEN.json`.
2. **Training**: `train <member> <seed> <cond>` saves model checkpoints to `ckpt/` and writes $e_{\text{on}}$ ONLY to `results/<cond>/<member>_seed<k>_pregate.json`. No $e_{\text{off}}$ is computed.
3. **Gates**: `gates` inspects all 40 pregate files, verifying G1, G2, G4a, and P6. Writes `results/GATES.json`.
4. **Evaluation**: `evaluate` runs ONLY after `GATES.json` verifies zero blocking failures. Computes E1 (rollout degradation $R$ and P3 repair ratio), E2 (algebra recovery and ALG-1 gate), and E3 (test-time splitting baseline). Writes `results/RESULT.json`.
